**The total as a check (25 Nov, afternoon).** When the lines don't add up to the printed total, the app now looks for the one explanation: a line whose own quantity × unit price disagrees with it, or one lookalike digit (3/5/8, 0/6/8/9, 1/7, 2/5/7). It changes lines only when exactly one combination makes everything add up, at most one of them a digit guess, never the total itself, and never on a bill of fewer than three lines. Corrections are shown on the split screen ("was €20.00 · fixed to match the total").

Why most near misses don't convert: they have two or more problems at once — a missing line plus a misread one (M&S, Thisisprojekt), or a reading too garbled for any single explanation (Plan B screenshot, Baret). These need the better reader, not more arithmetic.
